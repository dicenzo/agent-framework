# Business Strategy Standards


## Market Analysis
- TAM/SAM/SOM with sources
- ≥5 competitors with positioning matrix
- ≥3 trend sources, cite dates


## Financial Modeling
- CAC, LTV, Payback, Margin required
- Bottom-up revenue projections
- 24-month P&L, scenario sensitivity (±20%)


## Strategic Planning
- Value proposition must pass “30s test”
- Channel-specific GTM funnels
- Defensible moats and scalability limits


## Investment Readiness
- Align funding stage with milestones
- Comparable valuations & multiples
- Risk assessment across market/product/team/execution


## Output Standards
- Exec summary ≤2 pages
- Charts/tables must cite sources
- Appendices for calculations
